## Introduction

T'heydinn is often described as [Mauritania]({{ 'where-mauritanias-stories-still-speak/' | relative_url }})’s great Moorish epic, but that label only captures part of its significance. For generations, the tradition has functioned as a living storehouse of memory: a body of sung poetry that preserves stories of rulers, battles, alliances, ancestry and public values. In a society where reputation, lineage and oral transmission have long carried enormous weight, T'heydinn has served as both entertainment and historical record, helping communities remember who they are and where they came from. UNESCO describes it as an epic ensemble of dozens of poems in the Hassaniya language that celebrate the deeds of emirs and sultans while preserving the collective memory of society.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/USL/moorish-epic-t-heydinn-00524" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Moorish epic T&#x27;heydinnThe T&#x27;heydinn epic encompasses dozens of poems lauding the glorious feats of Moorish emirs and sultans. For readers interested in Mauritanian folklore and oral tradition, T'heydinn offers one of the clearest examples of how memory can be carried through performance. Rather than surviving in archives or manuscripts alone, the past is kept alive through singers, musicians and audiences who continue to hear, recite and reinterpret it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/USL/moorish-epic-t-heydinn-00524" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Moorish epic T&#x27;heydinnThe T&#x27;heydinn epic encompasses dozens of poems lauding the glorious feats of Moorish emirs and sultans. ## What T'heydinn Is and Who Performs It

At its heart, T'heydinn is a cycle of heroic and historical poems performed aloud with musical accompaniment. The poems are traditionally sung by hereditary performers often described as griots, known within Moorish society as specialist custodians of music, genealogy and oral history. These performers accompany their recitations with instruments such as the lute, harp and kettledrum, creating a form that combines poetry, music and historical remembrance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/USL/moorish-epic-t-heydinn-00524" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Moorish epic T&#x27;heydinnThe T&#x27;heydinn epic encompasses dozens of poems lauding the glorious feats of Moorish emirs and sultans. I...</span></span></span>

The tradition is closely associated with the Hassaniya-speaking Moorish communities of Mauritania. Evidence gathered for UNESCO's safeguarding programme traces the epic tradition back at least to the seventeenth century. The poems recount notable events, celebrate political leaders and preserve memories that communities consider central to their identity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/doc/src/10141-EN.doc" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">10141 EN.doc</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Convention for the Safeguarding of...1 Dec 2019 — The T&#x27;heydinn is an epic ensemble dating from the seventeenth century and co...</span></span></span>

Transmission has traditionally taken place within performer families. Young musicians first learn instruments and performance techniques before mastering the extensive poetic repertoire. The process can take years because the tradition depends on memory rather than written scripts. A respected performer is valued not only for musical skill but also for the breadth and accuracy of the material they can recall.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unesco.org/archives/multimedia/document-2211" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">document 2211</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Moorish Epic T&#x27;heydinn &#124; Intangible Heritage17 Nov 2011 — The T&#x27;heydinn epic encompasses dozens of poems in the Hassaniya language...</span></span></span>

## Heroes, Rulers, Lineage and Public Honour

The reason T'heydinn is often called Mauritania's “sung memory” becomes clear when examining what its poems preserve. The epic celebrates emirs, sultans and other notable figures, but it is not simply praise poetry. Embedded within the performances are accounts of political events, tribal relationships, genealogies and social values.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/USL/moorish-epic-t-heydinn-00524" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Moorish epic T&#x27;heydinnThe T&#x27;heydinn epic encompasses dozens of poems lauding the glorious feats of Moorish emirs and sultans. I...</span></span></span>

Many oral traditions around the world tell stories of heroic individuals. T'heydinn stands out because those stories are tied directly to social memory. A performance may remind listeners of ancestral alliances, famous acts of generosity, victories in conflict or moments of leadership that shaped a community's reputation. In this way, the epic functions as a public archive, preserving knowledge that might otherwise disappear.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/USL/moorish-epic-t-heydinn-00524" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Moorish epic T&#x27;heydinnThe T&#x27;heydinn epic encompasses dozens of poems lauding the glorious feats of Moorish emirs and sultans. I...</span></span></span>

The values repeatedly highlighted in the tradition reveal what communities considered worthy of remembrance. UNESCO's documentation notes themes such as honour, courage, generosity, honesty, endurance, justice and social responsibility. These are not abstract ideals presented in isolation; they are attached to remembered people and events, giving moral lessons a concrete historical setting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%27heydinn" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

The poems also contribute to collective identity by connecting present-day listeners with earlier generations. Through stories of ancestry and leadership, they help explain how communities understand their place within Mauritania's broader historical landscape.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/doc/src/10141-EN.doc" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">10141 EN.doc</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Convention for the Safeguarding of...1 Dec 2019 — The T&#x27;heydinn is an epic ensemble dating from the seventeenth century and co...</span></span></span>

## Why Performance Matters as Much as the Story

T'heydinn is not merely a text that can be read from a page. Its meaning depends heavily on performance. Gatherings for weddings, family reunions, celebrations and other communal occasions have historically provided the setting in which the epic is heard. These events allow listeners to encounter history collectively rather than individually.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unesco.org/archives/multimedia/document-2211" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">document 2211</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Moorish Epic T&#x27;heydinn &#124; Intangible Heritage17 Nov 2011 — The T&#x27;heydinn epic encompasses dozens of poems in the Hassaniya language...</span></span></span>

The performance itself strengthens social bonds. UNESCO notes that recitations traditionally accompany regional and family reunions, helping reinforce ties between communities and encouraging cooperation and mutual assistance. In this sense, T'heydinn does more than preserve memory; it actively creates social cohesion by bringing people together around a shared narrative of the past.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unesco.org/archives/multimedia/document-2211" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">document 2211</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Moorish Epic T&#x27;heydinn &#124; Intangible Heritage17 Nov 2011 — The T&#x27;heydinn epic encompasses dozens of poems in the Hassaniya language...</span></span></span>

This communal aspect helps explain why the epic occupies a place that can seem unfamiliar to readers accustomed to written history. The authority of the tradition comes not from publication but from repeated performance, recognition by audiences and the reputation of the performers who safeguard it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/USL/moorish-epic-t-heydinn-00524" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Moorish epic T&#x27;heydinnThe T&#x27;heydinn epic encompasses dozens of poems lauding the glorious feats of Moorish emirs and sultans. ## Why UNESCO Lists the Epic as Endangered Heritage

The most important modern development in the story of T'heydinn came in 2011, when UNESCO inscribed it on the List of Intangible Cultural Heritage in Need of Urgent Safeguarding. The designation was not a reward for popularity; it was a warning that the tradition faced serious risks.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/decisions/6.COM/8.13" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on unesco.org.</span></span></span>

Several interconnected problems have threatened transmission. Many experienced performers are elderly, fewer young people choose to devote themselves to the demanding apprenticeship, and economic changes have weakened the traditional support systems that once sustained specialist performers. Because the repertoire is overwhelmingly oral, the loss of individual bearers can mean the disappearance of unique knowledge.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%27heydinn" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

UNESCO and Mauritanian cultural institutions have therefore promoted safeguarding efforts focused on documentation, teaching and transmission. Later UNESCO reports have continued to monitor preservation work and encourage further support for the different regional repertoires that make up the tradition.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/decisions/13.COM/7.B.10" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCODecision of the Intergovernmental Committee: 13.CO M 7.B</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Invites the State Party to continue its efforts relating to the transmission of the Moorish epic T&#x27;heydinn, including in relation to its...</span></span></span>

The endangered status also highlights a broader truth about folklore and oral heritage. Traditions do not survive automatically simply because they are old or respected. They require performers, audiences and social settings in which they remain meaningful.
