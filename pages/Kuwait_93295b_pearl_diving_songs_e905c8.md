## Introduction

[Kuwait]({{ 'what-haunts-kuwaits-folklore/' | relative_url }})’s pearl-diving songs are more than old music. They are one of the clearest surviving links to the maritime world that shaped Kuwaiti life before the oil era. Long after commercial pearling collapsed in the 1930s, these songs continued to carry memories of dangerous voyages, exhausting labour, religious faith, homesickness and the tight social bonds that held crews together at sea. In a country where much folklore survives through stories of spirits, warning figures and family traditions, the sea songs preserve a different kind of memory: the lived experience of ordinary sailors and divers. Their importance lies not only in their melodies but in the way they keep a vanished way of life audible in the present.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/05367A7F22C344D8CE223F09DBE573C7/S0740155800013874a.pdf/kuwait-sea-songs-of-the-arabian-gulf-hamid-bin-hussein-sea-band-2014-music-of-the-earth-multicultural-media-mcm3051-recorded-annotated-and-produced-by-lisa-urkevich-32-page-booklet-with-notes-in-engli.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cambridge.org">[Cambridge University Press &amp; Assessment]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cambridge.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cambridge University Press &amp; AssessmentKuwait: Sea Songs ofthe Arabian Gulf: Hamid Bin Hussein...by S Hassan · 2017 — Despite the end of...</span></span></span>

The musical tradition often known across the Gulf as pearl-diving or sea music was shared by coastal communities in Kuwait, Bahrain and neighbouring states. In Kuwait, however, it became part of a broader cultural memory of the dhow fleets that once connected the country to trade routes stretching across the Indian Ocean. Today these songs are performed on heritage stages, recorded for archives and taught to new generations as symbols of national identity and maritime heritage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_of_Kuwait" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Music of Kuwait</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Music of Kuwait</span></span></span>

## What Do Kuwait's Pearl Songs Remember?

Unlike many forms of folk music that are detached from everyday work, Kuwaiti sea songs emerged directly from life aboard pearling vessels. They remember a world in which crews spent months away from home searching for pearls in the Gulf's waters. The songs evoke physical hardship, uncertainty and separation from families, but also collective resilience and pride.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aramcoworld.com/en/articles/2025/so25/songs-of-the-pearl-divers-drawn-back-to-the-surface" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aramcoworld.com">[AramcoWorld]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aramcoworld.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aramco World Arabian Gulf Pearl Divers&#x27; Songs Inspire Fusion Music</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Arabian Gulf Pearl Divers&#x27; Songs Inspire Fusion MusicSeptember 3, 2025 — 3 Sept 2025 — Pearl Divers and Their Songs Fijeri (se...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 3, 2025</span></span></span>

## How Work Songs Organised Life on the Dhow

The songs were not simply entertainment. They helped organise labour aboard sailing vessels. Across the Gulf pearl-diving tradition, particular songs accompanied rowing, hoisting sails, weighing anchor and other coordinated tasks. Their rhythms allowed groups of men to work together efficiently and maintain morale during long periods of repetitive effort.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aramcoworld.com/en/articles/2025/so25/songs-of-the-pearl-divers-drawn-back-to-the-surface" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aramcoworld.com">[AramcoWorld]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aramcoworld.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aramco World Arabian Gulf Pearl Divers&#x27; Songs Inspire Fusion Music</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Arabian Gulf Pearl Divers&#x27; Songs Inspire Fusion MusicSeptember 3, 2025 — 3 Sept 2025 — Pearl Divers and Their Songs Fijeri (se...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 3, 2025</span></span></span>

A key figure was the lead singer, whose voice guided the crew. He was answered by a chorus in a call-and-response structure that remains one of the most distinctive features of Gulf sea music. Hand clapping, drums and percussion provided a steady pulse that reinforced collective movement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aramcoworld.com/en/articles/2025/so25/songs-of-the-pearl-divers-drawn-back-to-the-surface" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aramcoworld.com">[AramcoWorld]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aramcoworld.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aramco World Arabian Gulf Pearl Divers&#x27; Songs Inspire Fusion Music</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Arabian Gulf Pearl Divers&#x27; Songs Inspire Fusion MusicSeptember 3, 2025 — 3 Sept 2025 — Pearl Divers and Their Songs Fijeri (se...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 3, 2025</span></span></span>

The tradition developed multiple song forms, each associated with particular situations or moods. Researchers describe a repertoire that includes several recognised genres, with some linked to activities on shore and others associated with life aboard the vessel itself. Rather than a single song style, Kuwaiti pearl music formed an entire musical system adapted to the rhythms of maritime work.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fijiri" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

This practical function helps explain why the songs survived so strongly in memory. Former divers and sailors often remembered them not as performances but as part of the physical experience of work: the sound that accompanied rowing, diving preparation and the daily routines of life at sea.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aramcoworld.com/en/articles/2025/so25/songs-of-the-pearl-divers-drawn-back-to-the-surface" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aramcoworld.com">[AramcoWorld]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aramcoworld.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aramco World Arabian Gulf Pearl Divers&#x27; Songs Inspire Fusion Music</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Arabian Gulf Pearl Divers&#x27; Songs Inspire Fusion MusicSeptember 3, 2025 — 3 Sept 2025 — Pearl Divers and Their Songs Fijeri (se...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 3, 2025</span></span></span>

## The Hamid Bin Hussein Sea Band and Preservation

One of the most important modern custodians of Kuwaiti sea-song heritage is the Hamid Bin Hussein Sea Band. The ensemble gained recognition through performances and recordings that documented traditional maritime repertoires at a time when the generation with direct pearling experience was disappearing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.urkevich.com/pearl-divers-music-kuwait" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: urkevich.com">[Dr Lisa - Saudi &amp; Gulf Heritage &amp; Music]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">urkevich.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Dr Lisa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Among the most lauded is the Hamid Bin Hussein Sea Band of Kuwait, who are featured on these recordings...</span></span></span>

Researchers have noted that the group's members are largely descendants of sailors and pearl divers. Their performances therefore represent more than historical reconstruction. They are part of an ongoing family and community tradition in which maritime music has been passed down across generations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.urkevich.com/pearl-divers-music-kuwait" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: urkevich.com">[Dr Lisa - Saudi &amp; Gulf Heritage &amp; Music]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">urkevich.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Dr Lisa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Among the most lauded is the Hamid Bin Hussein Sea Band of Kuwait, who are featured on these recordings...</span></span></span>

The band's recording *Kuwait: Sea Songs of the Arabian Gulf* became particularly significant because it brought Kuwaiti maritime music to international audiences while preserving detailed examples of the repertoire. The collection includes several traditional song forms associated with the pearl-diving era and has been discussed in scholarly reviews as an important contribution to documenting Gulf musical heritage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://music.apple.com/us/album/kuwait-sea-songs-of-the-arabian-gulf/873513543" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: music.apple.com">[Apple Music - Web Player]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">music.apple.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Imjailisi · 2:39; 2. Their work demonstrates an important feature of living folklore: preservation is not only about museums and archives. Traditions often survive because communities continue to perform them, adapting old material for contemporary audiences while maintaining links to inherited memory.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.urkevich.com/pearl-divers-music-kuwait" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: urkevich.com">[Dr Lisa - Saudi &amp; Gulf Heritage &amp; Music]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">urkevich.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Dr Lisa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Among the most lauded is the Hamid Bin Hussein Sea Band of Kuwait, who are featured on these recordings...</span></span></span>

## From Pearl Crews to Modern Stages and Heritage Revival

The collapse of the natural pearl industry transformed the social world that produced these songs. Economic change, the rise of oil wealth and modern urban life removed the original working environment in which sea music had flourished. Yet the songs did not disappear.
