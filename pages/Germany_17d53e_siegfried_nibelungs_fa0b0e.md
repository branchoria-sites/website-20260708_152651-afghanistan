## Introduction

[Germany]({{ 'germany/' | relative_url }}) is famous for fairy tales, but one of its most influential legendary traditions is not a fairy tale at all. The story of Siegfried and the Nibelungs belongs to the world of heroic epic: a darker tradition of dragon-slaying, royal rivalry, betrayal and revenge. At its centre stands Siegfried, a seemingly invincible hero whose death triggers one of the most devastating cycles of vengeance in European literature. The best-known version survives in the medieval *Song of the Nibelungs* (*Nibelungenlied*), written around 1200 from older legendary material that had circulated for centuries in oral tradition.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.worms-erleben.de/experience/entdecken-und-staunen/kulturprofile/nibelungen/nibelungenlied.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: worms-erleben.de">[worms-erleben.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">worms-erleben.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Worms ErlebenThe Song of the NibelungsThe best-known written source is the Song of the Nibelungs in Middle High German (dating from aroun...</span></span></span>

For generations, Germans treated the Nibelung story as a national heroic legend comparable to the role of the *Iliad* in Greece. Yet the tale is more complex than a simple celebration of heroism. It is also a tragedy about pride, deception, loyalty and catastrophic revenge. That tension helps explain why the legend remains culturally important long after the medieval world that produced it disappeared.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.uni-wuppertal.de/en/transfer/science-communication/jahr100wissen-/-100-years-ago/2024/nibelungenlied/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uni-wuppertal.de">[uni-wuppertal.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uni-wuppertal.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lies, betrayal and revenge: The Song of the NibelungsThe Song of the Nibelungs is a medieval heroic epic and was regarded as the national...</span></span></span>

## Why Siegfried Became Germany's Great Hero

The medieval poem presents Siegfried as a prince of extraordinary strength and courage. Before arriving at the Burgundian court, he has already achieved feats that place him beyond ordinary warriors. Later traditions emphasise his slaying of a dragon and his near invulnerability after bathing in its blood, leaving only a small vulnerable spot that eventually leads to his downfall. The poem combines these legendary elements with courtly politics and dynastic marriage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/song-of-the-nibelungs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: digitale-sammlungen.de">[Digital Collections]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">digitale-sammlungen.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Digital Collections Collection &#x27; Song of the Nibelungs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Digital CollectionsCollection &#x27; Song of the Nibelungs &#x27; - About &#124; MDZThe Nibelungenlied (The Song of the Nibelungs) is the most famous he...</span></span></span>

Siegfried's goal is to marry Kriemhild, sister of King Gunther. To win permission, he helps Gunther secure the hand of the formidable queen Brünhild through trickery and supernatural assistance. What begins as a heroic adventure gradually becomes a story about deception. Hidden resentments grow until the warrior Hagen learns the secret of Siegfried's vulnerable spot and murders him.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.uni-wuppertal.de/en/transfer/science-communication/jahr100wissen-/-100-years-ago/2024/nibelungenlied/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uni-wuppertal.de">[uni-wuppertal.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uni-wuppertal.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lies, betrayal and revenge: The Song of the NibelungsThe Song of the Nibelungs is a medieval heroic epic and was regarded as the national...</span></span></span>

Unlike many fairy-tale heroes, Siegfried is not portrayed as purely virtuous. Medieval and modern interpreters have often noted his arrogance, impulsiveness and overconfidence. These flaws make him a more complex legendary figure: admired for his courage but partly responsible for the chain of events that follows.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.uni-wuppertal.de/en/transfer/science-communication/jahr100wissen-/-100-years-ago/2024/nibelungenlied/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uni-wuppertal.de">[uni-wuppertal.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uni-wuppertal.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lies, betrayal and revenge: The Song of the NibelungsThe Song of the Nibelungs is a medieval heroic epic and was regarded as the national...</span></span></span>

### Kriemhild and the Tragedy of Revenge

If Siegfried is the epic's heroic centre, Kriemhild becomes its driving force after his death. Her transformation is one of the most striking features of the story.

Initially presented as a noble princess and devoted wife, Kriemhild refuses to forget Siegfried's murder. Years later she marries Etzel, the ruler known in the poem as the king of the Huns, and uses her new position to lure Hagen and the Burgundians into a deadly trap. The resulting bloodshed destroys nearly everyone involved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/song-of-the-nibelungs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: digitale-sammlungen.de">[Digital Collections]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">digitale-sammlungen.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Digital Collections Collection &#x27; Song of the Nibelungs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Digital CollectionsCollection &#x27; Song of the Nibelungs &#x27; - About &#124; MDZThe Nibelungenlied (The Song of the Nibelungs) is the most famous he...</span></span></span>

The second half of the *Nibelungenlied* therefore shifts away from heroic adventure toward relentless revenge. Readers expecting a triumphant national legend often discover something closer to a catastrophe. The story's power comes from watching honour, loyalty and vengeance consume an entire society.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.uni-wuppertal.de/en/transfer/science-communication/jahr100wissen-/-100-years-ago/2024/nibelungenlied/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uni-wuppertal.de">[uni-wuppertal.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uni-wuppertal.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lies, betrayal and revenge: The Song of the NibelungsThe Song of the Nibelungs is a medieval heroic epic and was regarded as the national...</span></span></span>

## From Oral Legend to Medieval Masterpiece

The *Song of the Nibelungs* did not emerge from nowhere. Scholars generally regard it as a literary reworking of much older Germanic heroic traditions that preserved memories of events and figures from the late Roman and early Migration Period worlds. These stories circulated orally for centuries before being written down.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.uni-wuppertal.de/en/transfer/science-communication/jahr100wissen-/-100-years-ago/2024/nibelungenlied/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uni-wuppertal.de">[uni-wuppertal.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uni-wuppertal.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lies, betrayal and revenge: The Song of the NibelungsThe Song of the Nibelungs is a medieval heroic epic and was regarded as the national...</span></span></span>

Around 1200, an anonymous poet in the Danube region transformed these older legends into a sophisticated Middle High German epic. The result blended ancient heroic material with the values and literary tastes of medieval aristocratic courts. The poem was enormously successful, surviving in dozens of manuscripts and fragments.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bavarikon.de/object/bav%3ABSB-CMS-0000000000004544?lang=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bavarikon.de">[bavarikon.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bavarikon.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">bav:BSB CMS 0000000000004544</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Manuscript C (Badische Landesbibliothek...</span></span></span>

This mixture of oral tradition and literary artistry is important for understanding the Nibelungs. The poem is not a direct record of ancient belief. Instead, it preserves legendary material while reshaping it for a medieval audience. It stands halfway between folklore and literature, making it one of the most important documents of German legendary culture.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.uni-wuppertal.de/en/transfer/science-communication/jahr100wissen-/-100-years-ago/2024/nibelungenlied/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uni-wuppertal.de">[uni-wuppertal.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uni-wuppertal.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lies, betrayal and revenge: The Song of the NibelungsThe Song of the Nibelungs is a medieval heroic epic and was regarded as the national...</span></span></span>

### The Manuscripts That Preserved the Legend

The legend survives through a remarkable manuscript tradition. More than thirty manuscript witnesses and fragments are known, with three major versions traditionally labelled A, B and C. These manuscripts are among the most important treasures of medieval German literature.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nibelungenlied" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 22, 2026 — In 2009, the three main manuscripts of the Nibelungenlied were inscribed in UNESCO&#x27;s Memory of the World Register in rec...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 22, 2026</span></span></span>

## How the Nibelungs Became a National Legend

For centuries the poem was largely forgotten. Its rediscovery in the eighteenth century coincided with growing interest in national history, folklore and medieval literature. Writers and scholars compared it to the *Iliad*, presenting it as evidence of a heroic German past. Siegfried came to symbolise courage, strength and national identity.
