## Introduction

The witches of Engolasters occupy a rare position in Andorran tradition because they stand at the meeting point of folklore and documented history. On one side are the famous stories of Lake Engolasters, where witches were said to gather at night, bathe in the water and punish anyone who dared watch them. On the other side are surviving court records showing that [Andorra]({{ 'why-andorras-mountains-feel-enchanted/' | relative_url }}) experienced real witchcraft prosecutions during the early modern period. Together, these two strands create one of the country's most distinctive supernatural traditions: a landscape haunted by legend, but also connected to the lives of real people who faced accusation before Andorran courts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitandorra.com/en/nature--sports/hiking-route-llac-d-engolasters/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitandorra.com">[Visit Andorra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitandorra.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit AndorraHiking route: Llac d&#x27;Engolasters... night to watch the witches bathe, naked, in the lake. Unlike many modern ghost stories that exist only in oral retellings, the Engolasters witch tradition can be examined from both directions. Folklore explains how local people imagined witches, while legal documents reveal how fears of witchcraft could have serious consequences in everyday life. The result is a fascinating case study in how belief, storytelling and justice became intertwined in the Pyrenean valleys of Andorra.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://patrimoni.gencat.cat/sites/default/files/hd-images-zip/descarregues/llibreBRUIXES_eng_final.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: patrimoni.gencat.cat">[Patrimoni Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">patrimoni.gencat.cat</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Patrimoni Cultural They were talked about</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">and they were out thereApril 22, 2021 — over twenty women in connection with at least nine cases of alleged witchcraft between 1514 and 1...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 22, 2021</span></span></span>

## Lake Engolasters and the Night-Bathing Witches

Lake Engolasters is one of Andorra's most famous legendary landscapes. The mountain lake lies above the central valleys and has long been associated with uncanny stories. According to local tradition, witches gathered there after dark, especially on moonlit nights. They bathed naked in the water, danced around the lakeshore and practised mysterious rites beyond the view of ordinary villagers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.encamp.ad/en/encamp-el-pas-de-la-casa/map/%40%40place_view/llac-dengolasters" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: encamp.ad">[Encamp]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">encamp.ad</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Llac d&#x27;Engolasters - Turisme Encamp i El Pas de la CasaIt is also said that on full moon nights, witches would gather at the lake t...</span></span></span>

The most widely repeated version of the story focuses on curious men from nearby settlements. Drawn by rumours of the witches' gatherings, they climbed to the lake at night to spy on them. The witches discovered the intruders and punished them with transformations, turning them into black cats. In some variants elsewhere in the Pyrenees, the punishment is petrification or another magical curse, but the Andorran versions most commonly feature black cats.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitandorra.com/en/nature--sports/hiking-route-llac-d-engolasters/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitandorra.com">[visitandorra.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitandorra.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit AndorraHiking route: Llac d&#x27;Engolasters... night to watch the witches bathe, naked, in the lake. The lake is also connected to another local legend: the tale of a village swallowed by water. In several retellings, a settlement once stood where the lake now lies. The inhabitants behaved arrogantly or refused charity to a stranger, leading to divine punishment. Floodwaters engulfed the community, creating the lake. The witches then adopted the newly formed waters as a gathering place. The combination of a drowned settlement and nocturnal witch meetings gives Engolasters an atmosphere unlike any other legendary site in Andorra.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.grandvalira.com/en/blog/lake-engolasters" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: grandvalira.com">[grandvalira.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">grandvalira.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">lake engolasters</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lake Engolasters in Andorra8 Feb 2024 — The story goes that one village was once completely engulfed by the water and that wit...</span></span></span>

One intriguing detail in modern versions of the legend is the claim that the witches disappeared at the beginning of the twentieth century. Local tourism materials connect their departure to the transformation of the area through engineering projects, including the construction of the dam and hydroelectric infrastructure. The story symbolically marks a shift from a supernatural landscape to a modern technological one.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitandorra.com/en/nature--sports/hiking-route-llac-d-engolasters/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitandorra.com">[visitandorra.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitandorra.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit AndorraHiking route: Llac d&#x27;Engolasters... night to watch the witches bathe, naked, in the lake. ## When Witches Moved from Story to Courtroom

The folklore of Engolasters might be dismissed as a colourful mountain tale if it were not for the fact that Andorra also possesses unusually rich evidence of actual witchcraft prosecutions.

Research based on records from the Tribunal de Corts, the principal judicial institution of the Andorran valleys, has shown that witchcraft accusations were not merely imaginary stories. Surviving documents indicate that more than twenty women were investigated in at least nine witchcraft cases between 1514 and 1522. These proceedings involved both secular and ecclesiastical authorities and demonstrate that fears about witchcraft formed part of real legal practice.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://patrimoni.gencat.cat/sites/default/files/hd-images-zip/descarregues/llibreBRUIXES_eng_final.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: patrimoni.gencat.cat">[Patrimoni Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">patrimoni.gencat.cat</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Patrimoni Cultural They were talked about</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">and they were out thereApril 22, 2021 — over twenty women in connection with at least nine cases of alleged witchcraft between 1514 and 1...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 22, 2021</span></span></span>

Modern studies of the archives have uncovered dozens of references to witchcraft prosecutions. Reports discussing the Andorran records describe around forty witchcraft trials conducted during the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries, making the country an important but often overlooked part of the wider Pyrenean history of witch hunting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://all-andorra.com/andorra-the-land-of-the-witche/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: all-andorra.com">[All Pyrenees]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">all-andorra.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">All Pyrenees Andorra, the land of the witches</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">All PyreneesAndorra, the land of the witchesMarch 6, 2019 — 6 Mar 2019 — The study revealed about forty witchcraft trials conducted by th...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 6, 2019</span></span></span>

The surviving evidence does not prove that the women accused at court were connected to the legendary witches of Engolasters. That link belongs to folklore rather than documentary history. What the records do show is that belief in harmful magic was taken seriously enough to trigger investigations, interrogations and legal proceedings. In that sense, the lake legends and the trial records illuminate the same cultural world from different angles.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://patrimoni.gencat.cat/sites/default/files/hd-images-zip/descarregues/llibreBRUIXES_eng_final.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: patrimoni.gencat.cat">[Patrimoni Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">patrimoni.gencat.cat</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Patrimoni Cultural They were talked about</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">and they were out thereApril 22, 2021 — over twenty women in connection with at least nine cases of alleged witchcraft between 1514 and 1...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 22, 2021</span></span></span>

### Real Women Behind the Fear

One of the most important lessons from the archives is that witchcraft accusations were not directed at imaginary beings. They affected real people, most of them women.

The Andorran records fit a broader pattern seen across Catalonia and the Pyrenees, where local courts often pursued alleged witches more aggressively than larger institutions such as the Inquisition. Accusations typically emerged from fears about illness, livestock losses, crop failures or unexplained misfortune. The accused were neighbours rather than supernatural creatures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://patrimoni.gencat.cat/sites/default/files/hd-images-zip/descarregues/llibreBRUIXES_eng_final.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: patrimoni.gencat.cat">[gencat.cat]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">patrimoni.gencat.cat</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Patrimoni Cultural They were talked about</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">and they were out thereApril 22, 2021 — over twenty women in connection with at least nine cases of alleged witchcraft between 1514 and 1...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 22, 2021</span></span></span>

This distinction matters when reading the Engolasters legends today. The beautiful and frightening witches who bathe beneath the moon belong to folklore. The women appearing in court records belonged to real communities and faced the consequences of collective suspicion. The two images became intertwined over time, but they should not be confused with one another.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://patrimoni.gencat.cat/sites/default/files/hd-images-zip/descarregues/llibreBRUIXES_eng_final.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: patrimoni.gencat.cat">[Patrimoni Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">patrimoni.gencat.cat</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Patrimoni Cultural They were talked about</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">and they were out thereApril 22, 2021 — over twenty women in connection with at least nine cases of alleged witchcraft between 1514 and 1...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 22, 2021</span></span></span>

## Why Engolasters Became a Witch Landscape

The association between witches and high mountain lakes is not unique to Andorra. Across the Pyrenees, isolated lakes, forests and mountain clearings were often imagined as gathering places for supernatural beings. Such locations lay beyond ordinary village life and therefore provided natural settings for stories about secret nocturnal meetings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://perennialpyrenees.com/2017/04/24/weekly-article-1-the-witches-of-engolasters-lake/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: perennialpyrenees.com">[Perennial Pyrenees]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">perennialpyrenees.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Perennial PyreneesWeekly Article #1 – The Witches of Engolasters LakeApril 24, 2017 — 24 Apr 2017 — Witchcraft and the Pyrenees have long...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 24, 2017</span></span></span>

Engolasters possessed several qualities that encouraged this reputation:

* Its elevated and relatively isolated setting.
