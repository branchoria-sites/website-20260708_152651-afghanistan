## Introduction

Among the most distinctive spiritual traditions of So Tom and Prncipe is Djamb, a complex set of beliefs and rituals centred on communication with spirits, ancestral memory, healing and protection. Rather than being a separate religion, Djamb operates within a wider cultural world where Catholic practices, African-derived spiritual traditions and local understandings of illness and misfortune overlap. In this worldview, the dead are not entirely absent from everyday life. Ancestors and other spirits may continue to influence the fortunes, health and wellbeing of the living, and ritual specialists help communities interpret and manage those relationships.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saotome-paradise.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Healing-And-Spirit-Possession-In-Sao-Tome-And-Principe-2p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saotome-paradise.com">[So Tom Paradise Tours]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saotome-paradise.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The term stems from the word nzambí or</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">São Tomé Paradise ToursHEALING AND SPIRIT POSSESSION IN SÃO TOMÉ AND ...September 23, 2025 — Traditional healers and ritual specialists,...</span></span></span>

For many So Tomeans, Djamb is important not because it offers dramatic ghost stories but because it provides explanations for suffering, family conflict, unexplained illness and social disruption. It is also a way of remembering the islands' African roots, especially the cultural traditions carried by enslaved and later migrant populations from Central Africa.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saotome-paradise.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Healing-And-Spirit-Possession-In-Sao-Tome-And-Principe-2p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saotome-paradise.com">[So Tom Paradise Tours]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saotome-paradise.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The term stems from the word nzambí or</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">São Tomé Paradise ToursHEALING AND SPIRIT POSSESSION IN SÃO TOMÉ AND ...September 23, 2025 — Traditional healers and ritual specialists,...</span></span></span>

## Spirit Possession and Communal Rituals

Djamb is most commonly described as a spirit-possession tradition. During ceremonies, participants may enter altered states of consciousness in which spirits are believed to communicate through them. Music, drumming, chanting, rhythmic movement and collective participation play a central role in creating the ritual atmosphere. Historical research links some Djamb practices to traditions brought from Angola and the Congo regions, where communication with ancestors and spirit possession were already well-established parts of religious life.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/02780771231222335" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journals.sagepub.com">[Sage Journals]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journals.sagepub.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sage JournalsCannabis Ecologies From Colonial Angola to So TomAmong the most powerful of those rituals was the djambi, drum performance...</span></span></span>

Anthropological accounts describe Djamb gatherings as both spiritual and social events. Some ceremonies focus on healing or divination, while others strengthen community bonds and reaffirm collective memory. The ritual space becomes a place where the living can seek guidance from forces believed to exist beyond the visible world.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/02780771231222335" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journals.sagepub.com">[Sage Journals]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journals.sagepub.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sage JournalsCannabis Ecologies From Colonial Angola to So TomAmong the most powerful of those rituals was the djambi, drum performance...</span></span></span>

Possession is generally understood not as random supernatural intrusion but as a meaningful encounter. A spirit may be thought to reveal the cause of illness, identify hidden conflicts within a family, warn of danger or express dissatisfaction over neglected obligations to ancestors. The possessed individual acts as a channel through which these messages become known to the wider community.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saotome-paradise.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Healing-And-Spirit-Possession-In-Sao-Tome-And-Principe-2p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saotome-paradise.com">[So Tom Paradise Tours]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saotome-paradise.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The term stems from the word nzambí or</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">São Tomé Paradise ToursHEALING AND SPIRIT POSSESSION IN SÃO TOMÉ AND ...September 23, 2025 — Traditional healers and ritual specialists,...</span></span></span>

## How Healing, Protection and Divination Work

Djamb healing practices are rooted in the belief that physical, emotional and social problems can have spiritual dimensions. Traditional healers, often known as curandeiros, serve as intermediaries between the human and spirit worlds. They diagnose problems, conduct rituals, prescribe herbal remedies and guide ceremonies intended to restore balance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saotome-paradise.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Healing-And-Spirit-Possession-In-Sao-Tome-And-Principe-2p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saotome-paradise.com">[So Tom Paradise Tours]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saotome-paradise.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The term stems from the word nzambí or</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">São Tomé Paradise ToursHEALING AND SPIRIT POSSESSION IN SÃO TOMÉ AND ...September 23, 2025 — Traditional healers and ritual specialists,...</span></span></span>

Healing is rarely viewed as purely medical. A person's suffering may be linked to offended ancestors, unresolved family disputes, harmful spiritual influences or breaches of moral obligations. The healer's task is therefore not only to treat symptoms but also to identify the deeper source of imbalance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saotome-paradise.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Healing-And-Spirit-Possession-In-Sao-Tome-And-Principe-2p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saotome-paradise.com">[So Tom Paradise Tours]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saotome-paradise.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The term stems from the word nzambí or</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">São Tomé Paradise ToursHEALING AND SPIRIT POSSESSION IN SÃO TOMÉ AND ...September 23, 2025 — Traditional healers and ritual specialists,...</span></span></span>

Protective practices are equally important. Objects placed around homes, ritual substances and prayers may be used to guard households against harmful influences. Such measures reflect a belief that unseen forces can affect daily life and that proper ritual action helps maintain safety and wellbeing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saotome-paradise.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Healing-And-Spirit-Possession-In-Sao-Tome-And-Principe-2p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saotome-paradise.com">[So Tom Paradise Tours]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saotome-paradise.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The term stems from the word nzambí or</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">São Tomé Paradise ToursHEALING AND SPIRIT POSSESSION IN SÃO TOMÉ AND ...September 23, 2025 — Traditional healers and ritual specialists,...</span></span></span>

Divination forms another key element of Djamb. Through possession, ritual interpretation or communication with spirits, practitioners seek answers to questions that ordinary observation cannot resolve. The goal is not usually fortune-telling in the popular sense but understanding why a problem has occurred and what steps are needed to restore harmony.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saotome-paradise.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Healing-And-Spirit-Possession-In-Sao-Tome-And-Principe-2p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saotome-paradise.com">[So Tom Paradise Tours]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saotome-paradise.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The term stems from the word nzambí or</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">São Tomé Paradise ToursHEALING AND SPIRIT POSSESSION IN SÃO TOMÉ AND ...September 23, 2025 — Traditional healers and ritual specialists,...</span></span></span>

## Why Ancestors Remain Part of Everyday Life

At the heart of Djamb lies a particular understanding of the relationship between the living and the dead. Ancestors are often regarded as continuing members of the community rather than distant historical figures. Death changes a person's status but does not necessarily end their involvement in family affairs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saotome-paradise.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Healing-And-Spirit-Possession-In-Sao-Tome-And-Principe-2p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saotome-paradise.com">[So Tom Paradise Tours]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saotome-paradise.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The term stems from the word nzambí or</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">São Tomé Paradise ToursHEALING AND SPIRIT POSSESSION IN SÃO TOMÉ AND ...September 23, 2025 — Traditional healers and ritual specialists,...</span></span></span>

This belief helps explain why remembrance is more than a symbolic act. Maintaining respect for deceased relatives can be understood as a practical necessity. Neglecting family obligations, forgetting ancestors or ignoring spiritual warnings may be seen as creating conditions for misfortune. Conversely, proper remembrance can bring protection, guidance and support.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saotome-paradise.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Healing-And-Spirit-Possession-In-Sao-Tome-And-Principe-2p.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saotome-paradise.com">[So Tom Paradise Tours]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saotome-paradise.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The term stems from the word nzambí or</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">São Tomé Paradise ToursHEALING AND SPIRIT POSSESSION IN SÃO TOMÉ AND ...September 23, 2025 — Traditional healers and ritual specialists,...</span></span></span>

The importance of ancestors also reflects the islands' history. So Tom and Prncipe was shaped by slavery, forced migration and cultural mixing. Many communities lost direct connections to ancestral homelands and lineages. Ritual traditions such as Djamb provided ways of preserving continuity with the past even when written records and family histories were fragmented or destroyed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/02780771231222335" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journals.sagepub.com">[Sage Journals]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journals.sagepub.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sage JournalsCannabis Ecologies From Colonial Angola to So TomAmong the most powerful of those rituals was the djambi, drum performance...</span></span></span>

## The Dead, Memory and Sacred Places

Djamb is closely connected to the landscape of memory. Certain locations may acquire spiritual significance because of historical events, burials, family histories or longstanding ritual use. These places are not necessarily formal shrines.
