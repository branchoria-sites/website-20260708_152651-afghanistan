## Introduction

Omani folklore is carried not only in famous legends about jinn, enchanted places and mysterious landscapes, but also in thousands of stories told in homes, courtyards and family gatherings. For generations, folktales were one of the main ways that knowledge, values and local memory travelled from elders to children. In many parts of Oman, women played a particularly important role in this process, preserving stories through everyday storytelling rather than through written texts. Recent research into Omani folklore has highlighted just how central female storytellers have been to the survival of the country's oral traditions. More than sixty collected folktales from across Oman have been traced to women narrators, especially elderly women who acted as cultural custodians within their families and communities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/omans-folklore-popular-beliefs-and-womens-oral-storytelling-ahmed-al-rawi/1147748005" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: barnesandnoble.com">[Barnes &amp; Noble]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">barnesandnoble.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Barnes &amp; Noble Oman&#x27;s Folklore, Popular Beliefs, and Women&#x27;s Oral Storytelling</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Barnes &amp; NobleOman&#x27;s Folklore, Popular Beliefs, and Women&#x27;s Oral StorytellingFebruary 5, 2026 — Offers a detailed analysis of more than s...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 5, 2026</span></span></span>

## What Do Omani Folktales Teach Their Listeners?

Traditional Omani storytelling covers a wide range of subjects. Some tales are humorous, some are cautionary, and others involve magical events, talking animals or encounters with supernatural beings. Although settings vary between mountain villages, coastal communities, oasis settlements and the far south of Dhofar, many stories share a concern with character and conduct rather than spectacle alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/78422166/An_Ethnographic_Discussion_of_Fairy_Tales_and_Folktales_from_Southern_Oman" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">An Ethnographic Discussion of Fairy Tales and Folktales</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">An Ethnographic Discussion of Fairy Tales and Folktales...January 1, 2019 — The tales, most of which were recorded in the 1970 s...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 1, 2019</span></span></span>

### Moral tales, animal stories and enchanted beings

Many Omani folktales revolve around practical lessons. Clever children outwit stronger opponents, generosity is rewarded, greed leads to misfortune, and patience triumphs over impulsive behaviour. These themes helped stories function as informal education long before widespread literacy and modern schooling.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mariellerisse.com/readers-guide-for-the-english-version-of-khadija-bint-alawi-al-thahabs-my-grandmothers-stories-folk-tales-from-dhofar/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mariellerisse.com">[Come From Away]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mariellerisse.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Come From Away“Reader&#x27;s Guide” for the English version of Khadija bint Alawi al</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">January 15, 2019 — These stories can be read as exciting folk tales, but the stories were meant to teach the listeners about their religi...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 15, 2019</span></span></span>

Animal stories occupy an important place within the tradition. Like folktales elsewhere in the Arab world, animals often speak, deceive, cooperate or compete in ways that mirror human society. Through these characters, storytellers could discuss social behaviour indirectly while keeping listeners entertained.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/397966618_Omani_Folk_Literature_and_Its_Impact_on_Society" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">397966618 Omani Folk Literature and Its Impact on Society</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ResearchGate(PDF) Omani Folk Literature and Its Impact on Society13 Nov 2025 — Omani folk literature represents a living mirror of the cu...</span></span></span>

Supernatural elements also appear frequently. Jinn, magical transformations, mysterious strangers and enchanted objects occur throughout Omani narrative traditions. Yet even these stories often return to practical concerns: respecting social rules, avoiding dangerous places, honouring family obligations or recognising the consequences of pride and dishonesty. The supernatural therefore serves as a dramatic framework for lessons rooted in everyday life.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/omans-folklore-popular-beliefs-and-womens-oral-storytelling-ahmed-al-rawi/1147748005" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: barnesandnoble.com">[Barnes &amp; Noble]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">barnesandnoble.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Barnes &amp; Noble Oman&#x27;s Folklore, Popular Beliefs, and Women&#x27;s Oral Storytelling</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Barnes &amp; NobleOman&#x27;s Folklore, Popular Beliefs, and Women&#x27;s Oral StorytellingFebruary 5, 2026 — Offers a detailed analysis of more than s...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 5, 2026</span></span></span>

These stories preserve distinctive regional perspectives while sharing broader themes found across Omani folklore, including moral testing, family loyalty and encounters with extraordinary beings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/78422166/An_Ethnographic_Discussion_of_Fairy_Tales_and_Folktales_from_Southern_Oman" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">An Ethnographic Discussion of Fairy Tales and Folktales</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">An Ethnographic Discussion of Fairy Tales and Folktales...January 1, 2019 — The tales, most of which were recorded in the 1970 s...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 1, 2019</span></span></span>

## Why Women's Storytelling Spaces Mattered

One of the most striking findings of modern folklore research is the importance of women in preserving Oman's oral heritage. While public poetry, tribal history and formal performance traditions often received greater historical attention, many folktales survived because women continued telling them in domestic settings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/omans-folklore-popular-beliefs-and-womens-oral-storytelling-ahmed-al-rawi/1147748005" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: barnesandnoble.com">[Barnes &amp; Noble]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">barnesandnoble.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Barnes &amp; Noble Oman&#x27;s Folklore, Popular Beliefs, and Women&#x27;s Oral Storytelling</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Barnes &amp; NobleOman&#x27;s Folklore, Popular Beliefs, and Women&#x27;s Oral StorytellingFebruary 5, 2026 — Offers a detailed analysis of more than s...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 5, 2026</span></span></span>

Storytelling commonly took place in family environments rather than formal venues. Children learned stories from mothers, grandmothers, aunts and older female relatives during evenings, household gatherings and periods of communal work. These domestic spaces became informal archives where narratives could be adapted, remembered and passed on to new generations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/omans-folklore-popular-beliefs-and-womens-oral-storytelling-ahmed-al-rawi/1147748005" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: barnesandnoble.com">[Barnes &amp; Noble]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">barnesandnoble.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Barnes &amp; Noble Oman&#x27;s Folklore, Popular Beliefs, and Women&#x27;s Oral Storytelling</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Barnes &amp; NobleOman&#x27;s Folklore, Popular Beliefs, and Women&#x27;s Oral StorytellingFebruary 5, 2026 — Offers a detailed analysis of more than s...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 5, 2026</span></span></span>

Because women often spent long periods caring for children and managing household life, they occupied a crucial position in cultural transmission. Researchers studying Omani folktales have concluded that women frequently carried a larger share of responsibility for transmitting stories and social values between generations. Interviews with Omani women from different regions reveal extensive narrative traditions that might otherwise have disappeared without documentation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/omans-folklore-popular-beliefs-and-womens-oral-storytelling-ahmed-al-rawi/1147748005" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: barnesandnoble.com">[barnesandnoble.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">barnesandnoble.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Barnes &amp; Noble Oman&#x27;s Folklore, Popular Beliefs, and Women&#x27;s Oral Storytelling</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Barnes &amp; NobleOman&#x27;s Folklore, Popular Beliefs, and Women&#x27;s Oral StorytellingFebruary 5, 2026 — Offers a detailed analysis of more than s...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 5, 2026</span></span></span>

These storytelling environments also gave women a voice within traditional society. Folktales could express concerns about marriage, family relations, social expectations, hardship and resilience. Although presented through fictional narratives, many stories reflect experiences and perspectives that were especially meaningful within women's everyday lives.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/396099816_Gender_Representation_in_Omani_Folktales_An_Analysis_Through_Kristeva%27s_Symbolic_and_Semiotic_Framework" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">guide the narration throughout four chapters.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Gender Representation in Omani Folktales: An Analysis...October 2, 2025 — 2 Oct 2025 — Omani traditions and mirrors the secr...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 2, 2025</span></span></span>

### More than children's entertainment

Modern readers sometimes assume folktales were aimed exclusively at children. In Oman, however, storytelling often involved mixed audiences. Adults listened as well as children, and stories could stimulate discussion about ethics, religion, family obligations and community behaviour. The storyteller's skill mattered as much as the plot itself. This performance aspect explains why oral tales are difficult to preserve fully in written form. ## How Oral Traditions Changed in Modern Oman

Like many oral cultures, Oman has experienced major social changes during the last half-century. Urbanisation, formal education, television, mobile technology and changing family structures have altered the circumstances in which folktales were traditionally shared. Researchers note that oral transmission has become less central to daily life than it once was.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://jcasc.com/index.php/jcasc/article/download/1714/234" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: jcasc.com">[jcasc.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">jcasc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Omani Folk Literature and Its Impact on Societyby O Siddig · 2025 — Folk literature plays a pivotal role in transmitting cultural heritag...</span></span></span>

The challenge is not that stories have vanished entirely, but that the settings supporting them have changed. Extended-family evenings and regular community gatherings are less common than in earlier generations, reducing opportunities for spontaneous storytelling.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://jcasc.com/index.php/jcasc/article/download/1714/234" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: jcasc.com">[jcasc.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">jcasc.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Omani Folk Literature and Its Impact on Societyby O Siddig · 2025 — Folk literature plays a pivotal role in transmitting cultural heritag...</span></span></span>

Scholars, cultural institutions and heritage organisations have increasingly documented folktales, oral histories, proverbs and traditional narratives. Recent studies have focused specifically on recording stories from elderly narrators before these memories disappear.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://journals.ekb.eg/article_444830_bd4bb803de5b567a1f35940fede1b2a8.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journals.ekb.eg">[EKB Journals]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journals.ekb.eg</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Journals[PDF] Preserving Omani Intangible Heritage: A Study of Folktale</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EKB Journals[PDF] Preserving Omani Intangible Heritage: A Study of Folktale...July 30, 2025 — Due to their oral tradition, folktales … t...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 30, 2025</span></span></span>

## From Spoken Stories to Collections and Classrooms

For much of their history, Omani folktales circulated almost entirely through speech. Today they increasingly appear in books, academic studies, school materials and heritage projects. This shift allows stories to reach wider audiences while also raising questions about how oral traditions change when moved into written form.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://journals.ekb.eg/article_444830_bd4bb803de5b567a1f35940fede1b2a8.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journals.ekb.eg">[EKB Journals]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journals.ekb.eg</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Journals[PDF] Preserving Omani Intangible Heritage: A Study of Folktale</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EKB Journals[PDF] Preserving Omani Intangible Heritage: A Study of Folktale...July 30, 2025 — Due to their oral tradition, folktales … t...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 30, 2025</span></span></span>

Recent scholarship has made a particular effort to document tales narrated by women. Researchers have collected stories from multiple regions of Oman, analysed recurring motifs and compared local versions with folktales found elsewhere in the Arabian Gulf and beyond. Such work demonstrates that Omani storytelling belongs both to a distinctly local cultural world and to a much wider network of traditional narrative forms.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/omans-folklore-popular-beliefs-and-womens-oral-storytelling-ahmed-al-rawi/1147748005" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: barnesandnoble.com">[Barnes &amp; Noble]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">barnesandnoble.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Barnes &amp; Noble Oman&#x27;s Folklore, Popular Beliefs, and Women&#x27;s Oral Storytelling</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Barnes &amp; NobleOman&#x27;s Folklore, Popular Beliefs, and Women&#x27;s Oral StorytellingFebruary 5, 2026 — Offers a detailed analysis of more than s...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 5, 2026</span></span></span>

The result is a growing recognition that folklore is not simply a collection of old stories. It is a record of how communities understood themselves. In Oman, women's storytelling spaces were among the most important places where that understanding was preserved.
