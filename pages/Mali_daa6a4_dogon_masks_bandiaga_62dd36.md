## Introduction

Dogon mask traditions are among the most famous ritual practices in Mali, but they make the most sense when understood together with the landscape where they developed. Along the Bandiagara Escarpment of central Mali, villages, shrines, burial places, meeting houses and ritual pathways are woven into a dramatic sandstone environment of cliffs and plateaux. Here, masks are not simply works of art. They are part of ceremonies linked to ancestors, funerals, community memory and the relationship between the living and the dead. The landscape itself is often treated as culturally charged, with sacred sites, older settlement remains and ritual spaces embedded in the cliffs and surrounding villages.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/516/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[unesco.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreCliff of Bandiagara (Land of the Dogons)The Bandiagara site is an outstanding landscape of cliffs and sandy p...</span></span></span>

For many international visitors, Dogon masks became famous through museum collections and anthropology. Yet in their original setting they belong to a wider ritual world in which performance, place and ancestry are inseparable. Understanding that connection helps explain why the Bandiagara region became one of Mali's most celebrated cultural landscapes and why debates about tourism, heritage and interpretation remain so important today.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/516/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[unesco.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreCliff of Bandiagara (Land of the Dogons)The Bandiagara site is an outstanding landscape of cliffs and sandy p...</span></span></span>

## Why the Bandiagara Cliffs Matter

The Bandiagara Escarpment is a vast sandstone formation stretching across Dogon country. UNESCO describes it as an outstanding cultural landscape combining dramatic geology with villages, granaries, altars, sanctuaries and communal spaces that have supported long-standing ritual traditions. It was inscribed on the World Heritage List in 1989 because of both its cultural and natural significance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/516/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[unesco.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreCliff of Bandiagara (Land of the Dogons)The Bandiagara site is an outstanding landscape of cliffs and sandy p...</span></span></span>

The cliffs are not merely a backdrop for folklore. They have shaped settlement patterns, social organisation and religious practice for centuries. Earlier peoples, including the Tellem, left traces in caves and cliffside structures that became part of local historical memory. Dogon communities later settled throughout the region, building villages on plateaux, at the foot of cliffs and along sheltered ledges. The result is a landscape where architecture, ritual and oral tradition overlap.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandiagara_Escarpment" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bandiagara Escarpment</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bandiagara Escarpment</span></span></span>

Many accounts of Dogon tradition emphasise that sacred places are distributed throughout everyday village life. Granaries, shrines, communal meeting spaces and ancestral sites are not isolated monuments but active parts of the social landscape. UNESCO notes that masks, rituals, feasts and ceremonies connected with ancestor traditions remain among the region's defining cultural features.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unesco.org/archives/multimedia/document-1162" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">document 1162</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cliff of Bandiagara, Land of the Dogons &#124; World HeritageJun 16, 2010 — The Bandiagara site is an outstanding landscape of cliffs an...</span></span></span>

## Masks, Funerals and Ancestors

The most famous context for Dogon masks is the funeral cycle often known as the dama. Although details vary between communities, these ceremonies are generally associated with honouring the dead and helping maintain proper relations between the living community and ancestral spirits. Numerous descriptions of Dogon ritual explain that masked dances accompany important funerary observances and are understood as helping the deceased complete their transition from the world of the living.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://face2faceafrica.com/article/fascinating-dogon-mask-dances-mean" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: face2faceafrica.com">[face2faceafrica.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">face2faceafrica.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Face2Face AfricaA look at the fascinating masks that bind Mali&#x27;s Dogon...Jan 31, 2018 — This dance ritual is performed to honour and fac...</span></span></span>

In these performances, masks are not treated as ordinary objects. Research on Dogon ritual traditions describes them as mediating between human and spiritual realms, while specialised knowledge governs their production, maintenance and ceremonial use. Some traditions connect mask practices with organisations responsible for preserving ritual knowledge across generations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/298728256_Inside_the_Dogon_Masks_The_Selection_of_Woods_for_Ritual_Objects" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ResearchGate(PDF) Inside the Dogon Masks: The Selection of Woods for...According to their religion, masks have a key role in traditional...</span></span></span>

To outside observers, the masks often appear striking because of their varied forms. Some represent animals, others embody ancestral or symbolic figures, and many carry meanings that become clear only within ritual performance. Museum collections frequently display the masks as sculpture, but in village contexts they are usually understood through dance, music, costume and ceremony rather than as isolated artworks.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://africaonlinemuseum.org/map/mali/dogon-dama-funeral/photos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: africaonlinemuseum.org">[africaonlinemuseum.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">africaonlinemuseum.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Africa Online Museum Dogon Dama Funeral » Photos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Africa Online MuseumDogon Dama Funeral » Photos - MaliDogon animal masks are regarded as beings of the bush, representing the world outsi...</span></span></span>

An important point often missed in popular descriptions is that the performance matters as much as the object itself. Songs, dances and ritual movements help communicate ideas about ancestry, social responsibility and continuity between generations. The masks become meaningful because they appear within a carefully structured ceremonial setting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.amherst.edu/system/files/media/1922/Masks%20and%20Mythology%20among%20the%20Dogon.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: amherst.edu">[Amherst College]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">amherst.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Amherst CollegeMasks and Mythology among the Dogon.pdf15 The songs and mottos pro- nounced during the funeral or dama ceremonies when the...</span></span></span>

## How Landscape and Ritual Work Together

The sacred geography of Dogon country helps explain why masks remain closely tied to place. Ritual life unfolds within a network of village spaces, shrines, meeting areas, burial locations and pathways shaped by the escarpment itself. The cliffs provide not only physical protection but also a framework for cultural memory.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.africanworldheritagesites.org/cultural-places/traditional-cultural-landscapes/bandiagara.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: africanworldheritagesites.org">[African World Heritage Sites]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">africanworldheritagesites.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">African World Heritage SitesCliff of Bandiagara (Land of the Dogons) (Mali)The Dogon brought their traditional religion and animist belie...</span></span></span>

Traditional Dogon settlements often include distinctive communal structures and ritual areas associated with ancestral traditions. UNESCO highlights the presence of altars, sanctuaries and communal meeting places as integral elements of the landscape. Rather than separating religion from daily life, the physical layout of villages incorporates ritual responsibilities into ordinary social space.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unesco.org/archives/multimedia/document-1162" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[unesco.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">document 1162</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cliff of Bandiagara, Land of the Dogons &#124; World HeritageJun 16, 2010 — The Bandiagara site is an outstanding landscape of cliffs an...</span></span></span>

The region's older archaeological remains add another layer of meaning. Cliffside caves and structures associated with earlier populations became part of local understandings of the past. In folklore terms, these visible traces help transform the landscape into a place of remembered ancestors and historical depth rather than merely a scenic environment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandiagara_Escarpment" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bandiagara Escarpment</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bandiagara Escarpment</span></span></span>

Because rituals, architecture and terrain are intertwined, many scholars and heritage organisations describe Bandiagara as a cultural landscape rather than simply a collection of historic buildings. The significance lies in the relationship between people, traditions and place.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://world-heritage-datasheets.unep-wcmc.org/datasheet/output/site/cliff-of-bandiagara-land-of-the-dogons" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: world-heritage-datasheets.unep-wcmc.org">[unep-wcmc.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">world-heritage-datasheets.unep-wcmc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">World Heritage DatasheetsCliff of Bandiagara (Land of the Dogons)The cliff and plateau of Bandiagara is one of the most impressive geolog...</span></span></span>

## Tourism, Museums and Famous Misunderstandings

Dogon masks became internationally famous during the twentieth century through museum collecting, photography and anthropological research. This visibility brought global recognition but also created misunderstandings. Many visitors encountered masks first as art objects in galleries, detached from the ceremonies and landscapes that gave them meaning.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://leiden.wereldmuseum.nl/en/whats-on/exhibitions/africa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: leiden.wereldmuseum.nl">[Wereldmuseum Leiden]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">leiden.wereldmuseum.nl</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Masked dances traditionally accompany key Dogon rituals linked to death and are performed by the fellowship of the...Read more...</span></span></span>

Tourism added another layer of complexity. The Bandiagara region became one of West Africa's most celebrated cultural destinations, and some villages became well known for mask performances presented to visitors. While tourism generated income and encouraged interest in Dogon culture, heritage organisations have also warned that commercial pressures can reshape traditions and place strain on vulnerable cultural sites.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandiagara_Escarpment" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bandiagara Escarpment</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bandiagara Escarpment</span></span></span>

Another recurring misunderstanding involves sensational claims about secret ancient knowledge, especially stories linking Dogon traditions to extraterrestrials or advanced astronomy. These theories attracted international attention but are not the reason Dogon mask traditions matter within Mali's folklore.
