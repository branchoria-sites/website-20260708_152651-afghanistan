## Introduction

Xooy is one of [Senegal]({{ 'where-senegals-stories-still-walk/' | relative_url }})’s most distinctive public ritual traditions: an annual Serer divination ceremony held shortly before the arrival of the rainy season in west-central Senegal. Rather than being a private act of fortune-telling, it is a large communal gathering where respected seers known as Saltigues deliver predictions about rainfall, harvests, disease, social tensions and other matters affecting community life. For many participants, the ceremony links spiritual knowledge, practical concerns and cultural identity at the moment when farming communities prepare for the most important season of the year. UNESCO recognised Xooy as Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2013, helping turn a long-established Serer tradition into a nationally and internationally visible symbol of living heritage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/xooy-a-divination-ceremony-among-the-serer-of-senegal-00878" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of Senegal The Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of SenegalThe Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the Se...</span></span></span>

## Why Xooy Still Matters Before the Rains

The timing of Xooy is crucial. It takes place immediately before the rainy season, when questions about weather, crop success, livestock health and disease become especially urgent. Historically, communities in the Serer regions depended heavily on agriculture and needed ways to interpret uncertainty in a difficult environment. Xooy emerged as a ceremonial framework through which concerns about the coming season could be expressed and addressed publicly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/xooy-a-divination-ceremony-among-the-serer-of-senegal-00878" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of Senegal The Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of SenegalThe Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the Se...</span></span></span>

In Serer tradition, the Saltigues are not simply entertainers or fortune-tellers. They are ritual specialists regarded as custodians of inherited knowledge and intermediaries between humanity, spiritual forces and the natural world. UNESCO describes them as “living mediums” who preserve and transmit knowledge essential to the ceremony and who help maintain harmony between people and their environment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/xooy-a-divination-ceremony-among-the-serer-of-senegal-00878" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of Senegal The Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of SenegalThe Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the Se...</span></span></span>

The ceremony therefore sits at the meeting point of folklore, religion and practical life. Predictions about rain are not isolated supernatural claims; they form part of a wider conversation about how a community should prepare itself for the months ahead.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/xooy-a-divination-ceremony-among-the-serer-of-senegal-00878" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of Senegal The Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of SenegalThe Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the Se...</span></span></span>

## What Happens During a Xooy Ceremony

Xooy is usually held as a long nocturnal gathering that can continue until dawn. Participants assemble around a designated ritual space while drumming, singing and ceremonial performance create an atmosphere of anticipation. One by one, the Saltigues enter the circle and deliver their predictions before the assembled crowd.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/xooy-a-divination-ceremony-among-the-serer-of-senegal-00878" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[unesco.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of Senegal The Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of SenegalThe Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the Se...</span></span></span>

Several elements make the ceremony memorable:

* **Public prophecy:** Predictions are delivered before a large audience rather than in private consultations.
* **Ritual performance:** Colourful clothing, symbolic objects, chants, songs and dances form part of the event.
* **Oral artistry:** Prophecies are often expressed through riddles, proverbs and dramatic speech.
* **Collective participation:** Audiences react, discuss and remember the forecasts long after the ceremony ends.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/xooy-a-divination-ceremony-among-the-serer-of-senegal-00878" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of Senegal The Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of SenegalThe Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the Se...</span></span></span>

The atmosphere is frequently described as suspenseful. Each Saltigue presents insights in turn, and the audience waits to hear what the coming year may bring. This combination of ritual authority and dramatic performance explains why Xooy remains one of the most visible events in the Serer cultural calendar.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/xooy-a-divination-ceremony-among-the-serer-of-senegal-00878" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of Senegal The Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of SenegalThe Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the Se...</span></span></span>

## Rain, Illness, Remedies and Public Prediction

Many traditional accounts of Xooy emphasise that the forecasts concern practical matters rather than abstract destiny. UNESCO’s description highlights predictions relating to rainfall, plagues, illness and remedies. The ceremony functions as a public forum where worries about health, agriculture and environmental conditions are voiced and interpreted.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/xooy-a-divination-ceremony-among-the-serer-of-senegal-00878" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of Senegal The Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of SenegalThe Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the Se...</span></span></span>

Rain occupies a particularly important place. In regions where successful cultivation depends on seasonal precipitation, questions about when rains will arrive and whether harvests will be abundant carry enormous significance. A favourable prediction can become a source of communal optimism, while warnings may encourage precautionary actions or ritual responses.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/xooy-a-divination-ceremony-among-the-serer-of-senegal-00878" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of Senegal The Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ICH UNESCOXooy, a divination ceremony among the Serer of SenegalThe Xooy divination ceremony is organized in village squares among the Se...</span></span></span>

Health is another recurring theme. Saltigues are traditionally associated not only with divination but also with knowledge of medicinal plants and traditional therapies.
