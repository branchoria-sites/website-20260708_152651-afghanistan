## Introduction

The 1880 Chiloé witch trial is one of the most unusual episodes in Chilean folklore because it sits at the point where legend became paperwork. Stories about witches, magical societies, secret caves and supernatural power had circulated across the Chiloé archipelago for generations, but in 1880 those beliefs entered court records when Chilean authorities investigated an organisation known as La Recta Provincia, also called La Mayoría. The resulting legal case created a rare archive in which folklore, local politics, fear, testimony and state authority became intertwined.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/wiki/portadaui_juicioalosbrujosdechiloe.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: memoriachilena.gob.cl">[gob.cl]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">memoriachilena.gob.cl</span><span class="citation-popover-title">portadaui juicioalosbrujosdechiloe</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Memoria ChilenaJuicio a los brujos de ChiloéEn 1880, el gobernador Martiniano Rodríguez decidió enjuiciar a la poderosa sociedad de brujo...</span></span></span>

For folklorists, the trial matters not because it proves supernatural claims, but because it preserves evidence of how people in nineteenth-century Chiloé understood power, justice and witchcraft. Many details now associated with Chilote witch lore survive not only in oral tradition but also in witness statements, judicial documents and later historical studies built around the case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/wiki/portadaui_juicioalosbrujosdechiloe.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: memoriachilena.gob.cl">[gob.cl]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">memoriachilena.gob.cl</span><span class="citation-popover-title">portadaui juicioalosbrujosdechiloe</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Memoria ChilenaJuicio a los brujos de ChiloéEn 1880, el gobernador Martiniano Rodríguez decidió enjuiciar a la poderosa sociedad de brujo...</span></span></span>

## When Chiloé Witchcraft Entered Court

By the late nineteenth century, Chiloé remained culturally distinct from mainland [Chile]({{ 'where-chiles-landscapes-become-legend/' | relative_url }}). The archipelago had long blended Indigenous traditions, Catholic influences and local beliefs about sorcery, spirits and magical specialists. Within this environment, stories circulated about a powerful secret society that allegedly regulated witchcraft across the islands. This organisation was known as La Recta Provincia.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/wiki/portadaui_juicioalosbrujosdechiloe.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: memoriachilena.gob.cl">[gob.cl]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">memoriachilena.gob.cl</span><span class="citation-popover-title">portadaui juicioalosbrujosdechiloe</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Memoria ChilenaJuicio a los brujos de ChiloéEn 1880, el gobernador Martiniano Rodríguez decidió enjuiciar a la poderosa sociedad de brujo...</span></span></span>

The investigation began in 1880 under Governor Martiniano Rodríguez. Rather than prosecuting people for supernatural powers themselves, authorities focused on the alleged existence of an organised association operating outside state control. Members were accused of forming an illicit organisation with its own hierarchy, officials and internal system of authority. The case unfolded during a period when the Chilean state was attempting to strengthen its institutions in peripheral regions and reduce competing forms of local power.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/wiki/portadaui_juicioalosbrujosdechiloe.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: memoriachilena.gob.cl">[gob.cl]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">memoriachilena.gob.cl</span><span class="citation-popover-title">portadaui juicioalosbrujosdechiloe</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Memoria ChilenaJuicio a los brujos de ChiloéEn 1880, el gobernador Martiniano Rodríguez decidió enjuiciar a la poderosa sociedad de brujo...</span></span></span>

The trial is sometimes described as one of the last major witch trials in world history. Unlike the famous European or Salem prosecutions of earlier centuries, it took place in an era of telegraphs, modern governments and expanding bureaucracies. That contrast is part of what makes the case so striking.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/into-the-cave-of-chiles-witches-20138093/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: smithsonianmag.com">[Smithsonian Magazine]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">smithsonianmag.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Smithsonian MagazineInto the Cave of Chile&#x27;s WitchesFeb 19, 2013 — First visited by Europeans in 1567, Chiloé was long known for piracy a...</span></span></span>

## La Recta Provincia and Local Power

According to testimony collected during the investigation, La Recta Provincia was said to possess a territorial structure that mirrored a government. Witnesses described districts, officials and leaders overseeing different parts of the archipelago. Some accounts even portrayed the organisation as a parallel authority capable of settling disputes and enforcing decisions beyond the reach of formal institutions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/wiki/portadaui_juicioalosbrujosdechiloe.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: memoriachilena.gob.cl">[Memoria Chilena]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">memoriachilena.gob.cl</span><span class="citation-popover-title">portadaui juicioalosbrujosdechiloe</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Memoria ChilenaJuicio a los brujos de ChiloéEn 1880, el gobernador Martiniano Rodríguez decidió enjuiciar a la poderosa sociedad de brujo...</span></span></span>

Folklore linked the society to a legendary headquarters at Quicaví, often described as a hidden cave known as the Casa Grande. In popular tradition this cave served as the meeting place of witches and the centre of their operations. Stories claimed that magical objects, secret books and ritual knowledge were stored there. The cave became one of the most enduring locations in Chilote mythology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warlocks_of_Chilo%C3%A9" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Warlocks of Chiloé</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Warlocks of Chiloé</span></span></span>

The organisation's legendary origins were also recorded. A widely repeated story claimed that the Spanish navigator José de Moraleda lost a magical contest against an Indigenous healer and surrendered a book of occult knowledge. Whether historical or not, the tale illustrates how local tradition explained the blending of Indigenous and European magical ideas. Historians note that the story belongs to folklore rather than verified history, yet its presence in the trial-era imagination is itself significant.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warlocks_of_Chilo%C3%A9" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Warlocks of Chiloé</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Warlocks of Chiloé</span></span></span>

## What the 1880 Trial Records Reveal

The surviving documents are valuable because they preserve details that might otherwise have vanished from oral tradition. Testimonies describe alleged ranks, duties, initiation practices and beliefs associated with Chiloé's witches. They also mention legendary beings and magical tools that later became central figures in Chilote folklore.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/wiki/portadaui_juicioalosbrujosdechiloe.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: memoriachilena.gob.cl">[Memoria Chilena]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">memoriachilena.gob.cl</span><span class="citation-popover-title">portadaui juicioalosbrujosdechiloe</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Memoria ChilenaJuicio a los brujos de ChiloéEn 1880, el gobernador Martiniano Rodríguez decidió enjuiciar a la poderosa sociedad de brujo...</span></span></span>

At the same time, historians approach these records cautiously. Court testimony does not provide a transparent window into belief. Witnesses were questioned by authorities who already suspected wrongdoing, and some later accounts suggest that coercive methods may have influenced confessions. As a result, the documents reveal a mixture of genuine local traditions, rumours, personal rivalries and official expectations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recta_Provincia" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Recta Provincia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Recta Provincia</span></span></span>

This ambiguity is precisely what makes the archive important. The records show folklore in motion rather than folklore frozen into a tidy story. People explained events through witchcraft, described hidden networks of power and connected everyday disputes to supernatural frameworks. The archive therefore captures not only legends but also the social reality of believing in those legends.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/wiki/portadaui_juicioalosbrujosdechiloe.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: memoriachilena.gob.cl">[Memoria Chilena]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">memoriachilena.gob.cl</span><span class="citation-popover-title">portadaui juicioalosbrujosdechiloe</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Memoria ChilenaJuicio a los brujos de ChiloéEn 1880, el gobernador Martiniano Rodríguez decidió enjuiciar a la poderosa sociedad de brujo...</span></span></span>

Several famous elements of Chilote mythology appear repeatedly in traditions associated with the case, including stories of magical flight, transformation, enchanted books and the feared cave at Quicaví. Whether every detail originated in the trial is less important than the fact that the proceedings helped preserve and circulate these motifs for future generations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warlocks_of_Chilo%C3%A9" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Warlocks of Chiloé</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Warlocks of Chiloé</span></span></span>

## Why the Archive Matters More Than the Verdict

The legal outcome of the case was significant locally, but the trial's larger importance lies in what it recorded. Many folklore traditions survive only because someone remembered them. In Chiloé, however, a substantial body of witchcraft lore entered official documentation. Researchers can compare oral narratives with court records and observe how stories changed over time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/wiki/portadaui_juicioalosbrujosdechiloe.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: memoriachilena.gob.cl">[Memoria Chilena]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">memoriachilena.gob.cl</span><span class="citation-popover-title">portadaui juicioalosbrujosdechiloe</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Memoria ChilenaJuicio a los brujos de ChiloéEn 1880, el gobernador Martiniano Rodríguez decidió enjuiciar a la poderosa sociedad de brujo...</span></span></span>

The case also reveals a clash between two systems of authority. On one side stood a modernising Chilean state seeking administrative control. On the other stood local traditions that interpreted social power through the language of witchcraft, ritual knowledge and customary authority. The trial transformed that cultural encounter into an archival record.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/wiki/portadaui_juicioalosbrujosdechiloe.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: memoriachilena.gob.cl">[gob.cl]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">memoriachilena.gob.cl</span><span class="citation-popover-title">portadaui juicioalosbrujosdechiloe</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Memoria ChilenaJuicio a los brujos de ChiloéEn 1880, el gobernador Martiniano Rodríguez decidió enjuiciar a la poderosa sociedad de brujo...</span></span></span>
