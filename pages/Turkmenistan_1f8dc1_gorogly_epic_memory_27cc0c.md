## Introduction

The Gorogly epic is more than [Turkmenistan]({{ 'turkmenistan/' | relative_url }})’s best-known legend. It functions as a shared cultural memory, a vast storehouse of heroic stories, moral ideals and historical imagination that has been passed from generation to generation through performance rather than books. At its centre stands the legendary hero Gorogly and his band of forty companions, whose adventures blend warfare, loyalty, justice, music, friendship and community values. For many Turkmens, the epic is not simply entertainment but a way of remembering who they are, what qualities they admire and how they understand their collective past. UNESCO recognised the epic art of Gorogly as part of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2015, highlighting its continuing role in identity, education and oral tradition.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/epic-art-of-gorogly-01028" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[unesco.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO ICHEpic art of GoroglyThe epic art of Gorogly is an oral performing tradition describing the achievements of the legendary hero Go...</span></span></span>

## The Hero and His Forty Companions

The core narrative revolves around Gorogly, a legendary warrior whose adventures are told across a large cycle of connected stories rather than one continuous tale. In Turkmen tradition, he is accompanied by forty loyal horsemen whose courage and devotion help him defend his people, overcome enemies and uphold justice. UNESCO describes the tradition as recounting the achievements of Gorogly and his forty cavalrymen, making the group itself an essential part of the epic’s identity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/epic-art-of-gorogly-01028" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[UNESCO ICH]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO ICHEpic art of GoroglyThe epic art of Gorogly is an oral performing tradition describing the achievements of the legendary hero Go...</span></span></span>

The hero is not remembered simply because he wins battles. He embodies qualities that listeners are encouraged to admire: bravery, honour, loyalty, fairness and leadership. The stories present an idealised world in which courage is balanced by wisdom and strength is linked to responsibility. In many episodes, friendship and trust are just as important as military skill. These themes help explain why the epic has remained culturally influential long after the social world that produced it began to change.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unesco.org/archives/multimedia/document-4025" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[unesco.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Turkmen Epic Art of Gorogly &#124; Intangible HeritageThe epic art of Gorogly is an oral performing tradition that describes the achieve...</span></span></span>

The Turkmen version also places strong emphasis on horses, companionship and communal solidarity. The hero’s famous horse and fortified stronghold are recurring symbols of independence and resilience. Rather than celebrating a lone champion, the stories repeatedly return to the idea that great achievements require loyal allies and a united community.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epic_of_Koroghlu" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Epic of Koroghlu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Epic of Koroghlu</span></span></span>

## Performance, Values and Oral Memory

What makes Gorogly distinctive is that it lives primarily through performance. Specialist singer-storytellers perform episodes using narration, poetry, song, improvisation and musical accompaniment. UNESCO identifies the tradition as a complex oral art form that combines storytelling and music rather than existing solely as a written literary work.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/epic-art-of-gorogly-01028" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[UNESCO ICH]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO ICHEpic art of GoroglyThe epic art of Gorogly is an oral performing tradition describing the achievements of the legendary hero Go...</span></span></span>

These performers, often known as epic singers or bards, are custodians of cultural memory. Their task is not merely to repeat a script but to recreate the story for each audience. Through performance they preserve language, poetic techniques and traditional values while keeping the narrative relevant to contemporary listeners. The tradition has historically depended on apprenticeship, with younger performers learning extensive repertoires and performance skills from established masters.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/doc/src/30311-EN.doc" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[UNESCO ICH]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">30311 EN.doc</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO ICH30311-EN.docThe &quot;Gorogly epic performers group&quot; of Turkmenistan is concerned community with the nominated element. The values embedded in the epic are repeatedly emphasised by both performers and cultural institutions. Bravery, honesty, friendship, fairness, hospitality and respect for others appear throughout the stories and are presented as ideals for community life. UNESCO specifically notes that the epic promotes these values while strengthening a sense of collective identity among its audiences.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unesco.org/archives/multimedia/document-4025" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Turkmen Epic Art of Gorogly &#124; Intangible HeritageThe epic art of Gorogly is an oral performing tradition that describes the achieve...</span></span></span>

This role as a moral and cultural guide explains why the epic has often been described as an “oral encyclopaedia” of Turkmen life. It preserves memories of social customs, relationships, expectations and ideals that might otherwise be forgotten. Rather than recording exact historical events, it records what generations of people believed was worth remembering.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/doc/src/24385.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[UNESCO ICH]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO ICHFree, prior and informed consent of practitioners and...The Turkmen epic art of Gorogly is an outstanding creation and oral en...</span></span></span>

### Why Oral Performance Matters

The power of Gorogly lies partly in the fact that listeners experience it collectively. A performance is a social event where memory is shared, reinforced and renewed. Each telling connects audiences to earlier generations who heard the same stories, even when details differ.

This process helps explain why the epic remains influential in an age of television, film and the internet. Written texts can preserve words, but live performance preserves relationships between storyteller, audience and community. The epic therefore functions as a cultural bridge linking past and present.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unesco.org/archives/multimedia/document-4025" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Turkmen Epic Art of Gorogly &#124; Intangible HeritageThe epic art of Gorogly is an oral performing tradition that describes the achieve...</span></span></span>

## From Epic Tradition to National Symbol

Over time, Gorogly has moved beyond the world of oral storytelling and become one of the most visible symbols of Turkmen national culture. The hero’s name appears in monuments, place names and public commemorations. Official cultural institutions describe the epic as a national treasure and a key expression of Turkmen spirituality and heritage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tdh.gov.tm/en/post/34195/epic-gorogly-epic-heritage-turkmen-people" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tdh.gov.tm">[TDH]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tdh.gov.tm</span><span class="citation-popover-title">epic gorogly epic heritage turkmen people</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The hero is depicted on Turkmen manats, several monuments have been erected in his honor...</span></span></span>

This transformation reflects a broader trend in which folklore becomes part of national identity. While the epic originated in oral tradition, modern Turkmenistan has increasingly presented it as a symbol of cultural continuity and historical resilience. The story serves both as a reminder of older traditions and as a statement about contemporary national identity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tdh.gov.tm/en/post/34195/epic-gorogly-epic-heritage-turkmen-people" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tdh.gov.tm">[TDH]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tdh.gov.tm</span><span class="citation-popover-title">epic gorogly epic heritage turkmen people</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The hero is depicted on Turkmen manats, several monuments have been erected in his honor...</span></span></span>

The emphasis was on safeguarding the tradition so that future generations could continue to perform, adapt and transmit it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/epic-art-of-gorogly-01028" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[UNESCO ICH]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO ICHEpic art of GoroglyThe epic art of Gorogly is an oral performing tradition describing the achievements of the legendary hero Go...</span></span></span>

## Why Gorogly Still Matters

Many countries possess famous heroic legends, but Gorogly occupies an unusually central place in Turkmen cultural life. It combines adventure, poetry, music and moral teaching in a single tradition that has survived for centuries through oral performance. The epic gives listeners memorable heroes, dramatic stories and ideals to admire, but it also serves a deeper purpose: preserving collective memory.

Its lasting importance comes from this dual role. Gorogly is both a story and a cultural institution. Through the hero and his forty companions, generations of Turkmens have expressed ideas about courage, justice, loyalty and community.
