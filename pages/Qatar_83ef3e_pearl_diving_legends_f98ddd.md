## Introduction

To understand [Qatar]({{ 'what-haunts-qatars-storytelling/' | relative_url }})’s folklore, it helps to begin with the sea. Before oil transformed the country, generations of Qataris depended on pearl diving, fishing and maritime trade. The Gulf was a source of wealth, but it was also a place of danger, separation and uncertainty. Out of that experience grew a rich body of stories, songs and memories that still shape how many people imagine the country’s past. Rather than focusing on supernatural creatures alone, Qatar’s maritime folklore often turns real experiences into legend: brave divers, skilled captains, long absences from home, storms, rivalries and narrow escapes at sea. These traditions survive not only in tales but also in music, museum collections, public art and heritage festivals that keep the memory of the pearling era alive.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitqatar.com/intl-en/about-qatar/traditions/pearls" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitqatar.com">[visitqatar.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitqatar.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Our rich pearling heritage continues to shine through museums, festivals, iconic attractions and local folklore.Read more...</span></span></span>

## Why Qatar’s Folklore Begins at Sea

For centuries, the sea provided the economic foundation of coastal Qatar. Pearl diving expeditions could last for months, with crews living in cramped conditions aboard wooden vessels and relying on skill, discipline and cooperation. Divers descended repeatedly without modern breathing equipment, often working at considerable depths while facing exhaustion and the constant risk of injury or drowning.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.qdl.qa/en/pearl-diving-inside-trade-shaped-gulf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: qdl.qa">[Qatar Digital Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">qdl.qa</span><span class="citation-popover-title">pearl diving inside trade shaped gulf</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Qatar Digital LibraryPearl Diving: Inside the Trade That Shaped the Gulf16 Jan 2018 — The divers who recovered pearl oysters from the bot...</span></span></span>

Such conditions naturally encouraged storytelling. In maritime communities, tales were not merely entertainment. They explained success and failure, celebrated courage, warned against recklessness and helped communities make sense of unpredictable events. A dangerous storm, an unusually successful season or a remarkable captain could all become part of local oral tradition.

Because so much of Qatar’s history was transmitted by word of mouth rather than writing, the line between history and legend often became blurred. Stories preserved memories of real people and occupations while gradually adding symbolic meanings about ingenuity, perseverance and social values. This is one reason maritime legends remain central to Qatari cultural identity even though the pearling economy itself largely disappeared during the twentieth century.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hbku.edu.qa/en/news/pearling-national-heritage" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hbku.edu.qa">[Hamad Bin Khalifa University]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hbku.edu.qa</span><span class="citation-popover-title">pearling national heritage</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Hamad Bin Khalifa UniversityPearling as National Heritage &#124; Hamad Bin Khalifa UniversityDec 7, 2020 — The celebration of pearling as part...</span></span></span>

## Pearling Before Oil

Pearl diving was not simply an occupation; it was the organising rhythm of life for many coastal families. Entire communities were tied to the annual diving season. Men departed for long voyages while women, children and older relatives remained ashore, waiting for their return and managing household life in their absence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.qdl.qa/en/pearl-diving-inside-trade-shaped-gulf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: qdl.qa">[Qatar Digital Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">qdl.qa</span><span class="citation-popover-title">pearl diving inside trade shaped gulf</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Qatar Digital LibraryPearl Diving: Inside the Trade That Shaped the Gulf16 Jan 2018 — The divers who recovered pearl oysters from the bot...</span></span></span>

This separation became one of the most powerful themes in maritime memory. Stories and songs often focus not only on the divers themselves but also on those left behind. The emotional strain of waiting for news from the sea became part of collective remembrance, preserved in songs and later commemorated in public artworks and cultural programmes. A recent public artwork on Doha’s waterfront, inspired by traditional maritime songs, explicitly recalls the feelings of families waiting months for loved ones to return from pearling voyages.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://qm.org.qa/en/stories/all-stories/art-as-memory-and-meaning-cultural-significance-in-qatars-public-art/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: qm.org.qa">[Qatar Museums]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">qm.org.qa</span><span class="citation-popover-title">art as memory and meaning cultural significance in qatars public art</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Qatar MuseumsArt as Memory and Meaning: Cultural Significance in...Apr 30, 2026 — The work evokes the rhythms of maritime life and the e...</span></span></span>

The importance of pearling also explains why legends associated with divers acquired lasting significance. They were stories about the central economic activity of the region and therefore became stories about community identity itself.

## Songs, Boats and Working Crews

One of the most distinctive elements of Gulf maritime folklore is the tradition of sea songs. These were not separate from everyday work. Singing coordinated rowing, sail handling and other demanding tasks while also strengthening morale during long voyages. A specialist lead singer often guided the performance, with the rest of the crew responding in chorus.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.qdl.qa/en/fann-al-ba%E1%B8%A5ri-great-art-sea" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: qdl.qa">[qdl.qa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">qdl.qa</span><span class="citation-popover-title">fann al baḥri great art sea</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Qatar Digital LibraryFann Al-Baḥri: The great art of the sea15 Jun 2017 — It seems that vocal genres performed by the women of the pearl...</span></span></span>

The songs carried practical and emotional functions at the same time. Their rhythms helped organise labour, but they also expressed longing, hope, hardship and pride. Different songs were associated with different stages of a voyage, from departure to diving operations and eventual return. Because crews repeated them season after season, they became a living archive of maritime memory.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/19/04/2024/katara-to-host-traditional-maritime-musical-contest-al-nahma-from-april-26" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thepeninsulaqatar.com">[thepeninsulaqatar.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thepeninsulaqatar.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">katara to host traditional maritime musical contest al nahma from april 26</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Peninsula NewspaperKatara to host traditional maritime musical contest &#x27;Al...19 Apr 2024 — The tradition of work songs associated wi...</span></span></span>

Women also maintained musical traditions linked to the pearling cycle. While men were away, women preserved songs and ritual performances connected to the opening and closing of the season. These traditions reveal that maritime folklore was not confined to the boats themselves; it belonged to entire coastal communities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.qdl.qa/en/fann-al-ba%E1%B8%A5ri-great-art-sea" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: qdl.qa">[Qatar Digital Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">qdl.qa</span><span class="citation-popover-title">fann al baḥri great art sea</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Qatar Digital LibraryFann Al-Baḥri: The great art of the sea15 Jun 2017 — It seems that vocal genres performed by the women of the pearl...</span></span></span>

## How Danger Became Legend

The sea’s dangers gave maritime folklore much of its emotional power. Divers descended repeatedly with minimal equipment, often relying only on physical endurance and experience. Every voyage involved uncertainty. Some crews returned with valuable pearls; others faced accidents, illness or disappointing seasons.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.qdl.qa/en/pearl-diving-inside-trade-shaped-gulf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: qdl.qa">[Qatar Digital Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">qdl.qa</span><span class="citation-popover-title">pearl diving inside trade shaped gulf</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Qatar Digital LibraryPearl Diving: Inside the Trade That Shaped the Gulf16 Jan 2018 — The divers who recovered pearl oysters from the bot...</span></span></span>

In such an environment, extraordinary stories flourished. Accounts of exceptional divers, clever captains and dramatic encounters with the sea were remembered and retold. Folklore transformed everyday risks into memorable narratives that taught lessons about courage, patience and skill.

Many stories also reflect a broader human tendency to personalise nature. The sea could appear generous one season and destructive the next. Legends therefore often portray it as a force requiring respect rather than domination. This theme remains visible in modern presentations of Qatar’s maritime heritage, which emphasise endurance and resilience rather than simple economic success.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nmoq.org.qa/en/calendar/tour-the-life-of-the-pearl-divers/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nmoq.org.qa">[National Museum of Qatar]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nmoq.org.qa</span><span class="citation-popover-title">tour the life of the pearl divers</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Museum of QatarTour: The Life of the Pearl DiversDec 7, 2025 — A tour designed for participants to explore Qatar&#x27;s pearl divers...</span></span></span>

## The Legend of May and Ghaylan as Maritime Memory

Among Qatar’s best-known maritime legends, the story of May and Ghaylan occupies a special place because it combines pearling, competition and technological innovation. Associated with the coastal town of Al Khor, the tale centres on a rivalry between Ghaylan, a powerful owner of pearling vessels, and May, a woman whose crews repeatedly outperform his. According to the story, Ghaylan eventually gains an advantage by developing the sail after observing a grasshopper’s wings, allowing his vessels to travel faster.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://qm.org.qa/en/stories/all-stories/hazawy-mai-and-ghaylan/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: qm.org.qa">[qm.org.qa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">qm.org.qa</span><span class="citation-popover-title">hazawy mai and ghaylan</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Qatar MuseumsHazawy Episode 1: Mai and GhaylanDec 21, 2023 — There are many myths and stories that came from the pearl divers of ancient...</span></span></span>

Whether understood as history, myth or a mixture of both, the tale reveals how maritime communities remembered innovation.
